## 1. Iran's Nuclear Infrastructure

### Natanz Enrichment Facility
- **Location:** Central Iran, Isfahan Province
- **Centrifuges:** 5,000+ IR-6 advanced centrifuges (IAEA 2025 report)
- **Uranium Enrichment Level:** 60% (weapons-grade is 90%)
- **Stockpile:** Enough enriched uranium for 9 nuclear weapons (Dec 2024 IAEA)
- **Underground:** 8 meters underground with concrete/steel shielding

### Fordow Fuel Enrichment Plant
- **Location:** Near Qom, buried inside mountain
- **Depth:** 80-90 meters underground
- **Shielding:** Natural rock + reinforced concrete
- **GBU-57 MOP Penetration:** ~60 meters maximum → **Cannot reach Fordow**

### Isfahan Uranium Conversion Facility
- Converts yellowcake (UF₄) to UF₆ gas for centrifuge enrichment
- Above-ground but heavily fortified

## 2. US Nuclear Arsenal Details

### B61-12 Tactical Nuclear Bomb
- **Yield:** 0.3 to 50 kilotons (dial-a-yield)
- **Type:** Earth-penetrating guided nuclear bomb
- **Delivery:** B-2 Spirit, F-35, F-15E
- **Accuracy:** 30m CEP (GPS-guided)
- **Comparison:** Hiroshima = 15 kilotons; B61-12 max = 50 kilotons = **3.3x Hiroshima**

### GBU-57 Massive Ordnance Penetrator (MOP)
- **Weight:** 13,600 kg (30,000 lbs)
- **Penetration:** ~60 meters of reinforced concrete
- **Used on:** B-2 Spirit bomber
- **Limitation:** Cannot reach Fordow's 80m depth

### B-2 Spirit Stealth Bomber
- **Wingspan:** 52.4 meters
- **Radar Cross-Section:** Size of a large bird
- **Range:** 11,000 km (unrefueled)
- **Base:** Whiteman AFB, Missouri / Diego Garcia

## 7. Nuclear Winter Science

### Rutgers University Study (2022)
- **Scenario:** India-Pakistan limited nuclear war (~100 Hiroshima-size bombs)
- **Soot injected:** 5-10 million tons into stratosphere
- **Sunlight blocked:** 20-35% reduction globally
- **Temperature drop:** 2-3°C global average
- **Crop yield reduction:** 40% for wheat, rice, corn
- **Duration:** Effects last 5-10 years
- **People at risk of starvation:** 2+ billion

### Full-Scale Nuclear War Scenario (US-Russia)
- **Temperature drop:** 8-10°C
- **Growing season:** Eliminated entirely for 3-5 years
- **Starvation deaths:** 5+ billion projected
