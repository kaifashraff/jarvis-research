# Voiceover Text — Clean Version
**Video:** "72 Ghante Jo Duniya Badal Denge"
**Duration:** ~12-15 min

## HOOK (0:00 – 1:00)

2020 mein America ne Iran ke sabse powerful general ko maut ke ghaat utar diya. Poori duniya ne socha — ab toh World War 3 pakka hai. Lekin... kuch nahi hua. Dono sides ne aankh milayi — aur palat gayi. Jaise do wrestler ring ke beech khade hon, saansein bhaari hon, crowd cheekh rahi ho... par koi pehla punch na maare.

2025. Iran ne 60% enriched uranium produce kiya. Israel ne B-2 bombers deploy kiye. America ne 7th Fleet Gulf mein bhej di.

Aaj... 2026 mein... woh wrestler jo 5 saal se ek dusre ko ghoor rahe the... unhone gloves utaar diye. Aur is baar... yeh punch nahi hoga. Yeh atomic hoga.

---

## THE STAGE (1:00 – 3:00)

Imagine karo India ek baar mein 100 Taj Mahal build kar raha hai. Itna paisa, itna waqt, itna secret... Iran ne apne nuclear program mein invest kiya hai.

Natanz. 5,000 IR-6 centrifuges. 1,000 times per second. Aur yeh ek cheez bana rahi hain — enriched uranium. Woh material jo ek nuclear bomb ki jaan hai.

Lekin Natanz toh bas practice tha. Asli khazana hai — Fordow. 80 meter gehra. Utne deep jitni 25-floor building lambi hai. Zameen ke neeche. Concrete, steel, natural rock.

America ka GBU-57 bunker buster missile — 60 meter penetrate karta hai. Fordow tak pahunch nahi sakta.

Socho — tumhare paas duniya ka sabse powerful bomb hai... aur ek mountain tumhe chidha raha hai. Toh tum kya karoge?

## THE FIRST STRIKE (3:00 – 5:30)

Raat ke 2:47 baje. Whiteman Air Force Base, Missouri. B-2 Spirit quietly takeoff karta hai. Wingspan — 52 meter. Radar pe ek bird se chhota. Aur iske andar — ek aisa weapon jo Hiroshima ke baad pehli baar use hoga.

B61-12. America ka latest tactical nuclear bomb. 50 kilotons. Hiroshima ka bomb tha 15 kilotons. 3.3 guna zyada.

Socho — Hiroshima ne ek poore ko khaali kar diya tha. Aur yeh usse 3 guna bada hai.

Yeh bomb ghusta hai. Zameen ko chheerta hua — 30 meter tak — phir... underground detonation. Zameen se ek volcano jaisa — mitti, radiation, molten rock 500 meter hawa mein udti hai. Jaise koi ne zameen ke andar ek dragon ko jaga diya ho.

Fordow ke andar — 80 meter neeche — koi escape nahi tha. Shockwave ne tunnels seal kar diye. Andar ka oxygen fire ne consume kar liya. Ek facility — 10 saal ki mehnat — 12 second mein.

Lekin yeh sirf pehla attack tha. Aur jab pehla domino girta hai... toh baaki bhi girte hain.

---

## IRAN STRIKES BACK (5:30 – 8:00)

Iran ka reply aaya — aur kya reply.

2,000 se zyada ballistic missiles ek saath. Range — 2,000+ kilometers. Matlab — Tel Aviv, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Riyadh — sab direct range mein. Aur yeh sirf missiles nahi hain. Yeh 40 saal ka geopolitical gussa hai. Sab ek saath launch hoga.

Ab suno sabse darrawani cheez. Fattah. Hypersonic glide vehicle. 15 Mach. Yani 18,000 km/h. Ek bullet ka speed 3,000 km/h hota hai. Yeh usse 6 guna tez hai. Aur sabse scary part? Yeh mid-air mein direction change kar sakta hai. Matlab — jo missile defense system use pakde — woh use guess karega. Aur jab aapko guess karna pade... toh aap already haar chuke ho.

Israel ke paas hai Iron Dome. Duniya ka sabse advanced missile defense. 90% success rate. Lekin 90% matlab — agar 1,000 missiles aayein, toh 100 missiles through jaayenge. Aur Hezbollah Lebanon se ek saath ek lakh pannaas hazaar rockets fire karega. Iron Dome ko itna load hoga ki woh crash ho jayega — literally software crash.

Aur ab woh cheez jo aapke pocket ko seedha maaregi. Strait of Hormuz. Ek patla sa paani ka rasta — 33 kilometer chauraa. Lekin iske through jaati hai — duniya ki 20% oil supply. Iran ne isko lock kar diya. Naval mines. Speedboats. Anti-ship missiles. Koi tanker andar jaayega — toh bahar nahi aayega.

Socho — aapke city ka petrol pump khaali ho raha hai. Ek din. Do din. Teesre din — lines. Chauthe din — ₹180 per liter. Paanchve din — ₹250. Aur aapka auto-wala bhaiya bolta hai — bhai, diesel nahi mil raha, kal se nahi chal paunga. Yeh shuruwat hai.

## DOMINO EFFECT (8:00 – 10:30)

Ab yeh sirf Middle East ka issue nahi raha. Yeh aapka issue hai. Mera issue hai. Har ek insaan ka issue hai.

India. Hum 85% oil import karte hain — Middle East se. Hormuz block hua — supply ruki. Petrol ₹280 per liter. Diesel bhi. Matlab — trucks band. Trains bhi. Trucks band — toh mandi mein sabzi nahi. Sabzi nahi — toh pyaz ₹200 per kilo. Aur jab pyaz ₹200 ka ho... toh samajh lo duniya badal gayi.

Stock market — Sensex 80,000 se seedha 45,000 pe aayega. 44% crash. 2008 se bhi bada. FII paisa nikaal le jaayenge — America, gold, Bitcoin mein. Rupee — 83 se seedha ₹110 per dollar. Import mehenga. Export band. Aur jo startups funding pe chal rahe hain — unka Series A ab Series "Are Yaar" ban jaayega.

Aur ab woh cheez jo actually scary hai. Nuclear winter. Rutgers University ne 2022 mein study publish kiya — multiple nuclear explosions = 5 se 10 million tons soot stratosphere mein jaayega. Socho — ek chhata itna bada ki poori duniya pe chaa jaaye. Yeh soot sunlight ko block karega. 2 saal tak. Global temperature 2 se 3°C drop hogi. Crop yields 40% kam. Wheat, rice, corn — sabko asar. Aur jab kheti nahi hogi... toh 2 billion logon ko bhookh lagne lagti hai. Yeh exaggeration nahi — science hai.

## THE NEW WORLD (10:30 – 12:30)

Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty — jo 1970 se chal raha tha, jo 191 countries ne sign kiya tha — woh khatam. Ek document... 56 saal ki diplomacy... ek din mein.

Saudi Arabia ne kaha — Iran ke paas tha. Ab hum nahi banayenge? Toh kya hum mar jaayenge? Turkey ne bola — Turkey deserves deterrence. Egypt — Nile ke paas secret facility. Japan — jo 80 saal se atomic bomb ka victim raha hai — ab woh khud bomb banayega. South Korea — North Korea pehle se hai, ab South bhi. Aur suddenly — duniya mein 3 nuclear powers nahi... 12 nuclear powers hain. Welcome to the new Cold War. Except yeh cold nahi. Yeh boiling hai.

Iran ki population 9 crore. Expert estimates kehte hain — 3 se 5 million log displaced ho jaayenge. 2015 Syrian crisis ne Europe ko hila diya tha. Usmein 1.3 million refugees the. Yeh usse 30 guna bada hoga. Turkey, Europe, Pakistan, India — sabki borders pe pressure. Aur India? Western border pe Pakistan ek nuclear state hai. Agar Pakistan ko lage ki India ne Iran attack support kiya — toh next hum hain — toh woh first-strike ke baare mein sochne lagta hai. Aur woh sochna... sabse dangerous cheez hai.

## THE TRUTH (12:30 – 14:00)

Ab sach bataun? Main aapko yeh nahi bata raha ki nuclear war bura hai. Yeh toh har bachcha jaanta hai. Main aapko yeh bata raha hoon ki nuclear war mein koi nahi bachta. Winner nahi hota. Survivors hote hain — jo radiation se marte hain, bhookh se marte hain, ya us bhookh se ladte hain jo unke bacchon ko kha rahi hai.

Jis pilot ne bomb drop kiya — usne socha hoga ki main desh ki raksha kar raha hoon. Aur jo maa apne bacche ko leke bhaag rahi hai — uske liye woh bomber ek demon hai. Dono sahi hain. Dono galat hain. Aur yahi nuclear war ka sabse bada jhooth hai — ki yeh war hai. War mein winner hota hai. Yeh suicide hai — jisme sab mar jaate hain.

1955 mein Albert Einstein ne kaha: I know not with what weapons World War III will be fought... but World War IV will be fought with sticks and stones.

70 saal beet gaye. Aur aaj ke weapons... Einstein ke imagination se bhi zyada darrawne hain. Duniya ke paas kul 12,500 nuclear weapons hain. Inme se jitne 10% use honge... itne hain poori duniya ko baar baar destroy karne ke liye. Aur question yeh nahi hai ki kaun jeetega. Question yeh hai...

Kaun... bachega?
