## 🎯 **3-STEP FIX (Hinglish mein)**

### **STEP 1: PRICING STRATEGY — Apna Price Badhao**

**✅ Charm Pricing:**
- **₹4,999** instead of ₹5,000
- **Why?** Customers ₹1 kam samajhte hain, lekin tumhara profit ₹1 zyada rehta hai
- **Result:** 7% zyada conversion rate + 7% zyada profit per sale

**✅ Anchor Pricing:**
- **Show:** ₹7,999
- **Sale:** ₹5,499 (30% discount)
- **Why?** Customer ko lagega "30% off" — lekin tumhara margin 40% rehta hai
- **Example:** Tum ₹3,000 ka zari kaam karte ho → ₹4,200 pe becho

**✅ Bundle Pricing:**
- **Zari + Silai combo:** 15% discount
- **Why?** Customer ek baar mein do services lega → tumhara revenue double
- **Example:** Zari work ₹5,000 + Silai ₹3,000 = ₹8,000 → Bundle at ₹6,800

**✅ Premium Tier:**
- **Bridal Collection:** 3x regular price
- **Why?** Brides premium pay karne ko taiyar hain
- **Example:** ₹15,000 ka zari work → ₹45,000 pe becho

---

### **STEP 2: COST CONTROL — Kharcha Kam Kar**

**🔥 Gold Rate Badhta Hai?**
- **Buffer Add Kar:** Quotation mein 5-10% buffer rakho
- **Example:** Gold ₹75,200 → Quotation ₹80,000 likho
- **Why?** Agar gold ₹77,000 ho jaaye → tumhara profit ₹3,000 rehta hai

**🔥 Bulk Purchase Kar:**
- **Zari Thread:** 3-month stock khareedo — 5% discount mil sakta hai
- **Why?** 5% discount = ₹3,760 bachao per 10gm

**🔥 Karigar Efficiency:**
- **Per Hour Rate Badhao:** ₹50 → ₹70
- **Why?** Agar tumhara karigar 10 hours kaam kare → tumhara cost ₹700 → tumhara price ₹1,200
- **Profit:** ₹500 per order
