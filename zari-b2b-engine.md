### AGENT 3: VALIDATION_AGENT (Truth Checker)
**Tasks:**
- Cross-check demand using Google Trends

- Remove fake or low-quality leads

### AGENT 4: OFFER_AGENT (Conversion Strategist)
**Tasks:**
- Irresistible B2B offer (low MOQ, custom design, fast delivery)

- WhatsApp + Email outreach scripts

### AGENT 5: EXECUTION_AGENT (Revenue Generator)
**Tasks:**
- Send 100 personalized outreach messages daily
- Track replies and conversions
- Optimize messaging based on response rate

### AGENT 6: CRITIC_AGENT (Optimizer)
**Tasks:**
- Analyze why deals fail
- Improve targeting and messaging
- Increase conversion rate week by week
