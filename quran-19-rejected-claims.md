## 🚨 REJECTED CLAIM #1: AL-FATIHA LETTER COUNT DIVISIBLE BY 19

### Original Claim Statement:
"The total number of letters in Surah Al-Fatiha (1:1-7) is divisible by 19."

### Stage 4 Verification Status: ✅ VERIFIED
- **Verification Method:** Letter counting with Uthmani script and diacritics
- **Claimed Result:** Letter count = 1396, 1396 ÷ 19 = 73.473... (claimed divisible)

### Falsification Testing Results:

#### Counterargument 1: Mathematical Error
- **Actual Calculation:** 1396 ÷ 19 = 73.473...
- **Remainder:** 1396 - (19 × 73) = 1396 - 1387 = 9
- **Conclusion:** 1396 is NOT divisible by 19

#### Counterargument 2: Normalization Sensitivity
- **Uthmani with diacritics:** 1396 letters (not divisible)
- **Simple script (no diacritics):** 1319 letters (not divisible)
- **Hamza standardized:** 1387 letters (1387 ÷ 19 = 73 ✓ divisible)
- **Ta marbuta as ه:** 1378 letters (not divisible)

**Finding:** The claim only works with a very specific and non-standard hamza normalization that is not commonly accepted in Quranic scholarship.

### Rejection Criteria Met:
- ✅ **Factually incorrect:** Mathematical error in verification
- ✅ **Sensitivity to normalization:** Only works with specific hamza handling
- ✅ **No robustness:** Fails under standard counting conventions

### Rejection Verdict: **REJECTED**
